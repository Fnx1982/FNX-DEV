3. Purchase & Refund Policy

3.1 How purchasing works

You choose a plan, fill in the registration form, review the order summary (plan, details and total price), accept the policies and pay through Stripe's secure checkout. Clients outside Brazil pay in US dollars by international card. Clients in Brazil pay in reais (card, boleto and, when available, Pix). The purchase is confirmed when payment is approved, and you receive the confirmation by email.

3.2 7-day guarantee

You may cancel your purchase within 7 (seven) calendar days of payment confirmation and receive 100% of the amount paid, no questions asked.

3.3 What happens during the 7 days

During the guarantee period we run the briefing and start development, with previews for you to follow. Launch, delivery of files/code and transfer of access happen only after the 7-day period ends. If you cancel within the period, you get a full refund and none of the work produced is delivered or may be used.

3.4 How to request a refund

  • Email suporte.fnxtech@gmail.com, reply to the purchase confirmation email or use the website contact form (subject "Refund / cancellation") within the period, stating the email used at checkout;
  • You receive confirmation that we got your cancellation request;
  • We request the refund from Stripe immediately so it can notify your card issuer or bank.

3.5 How the money is returned

  • International card: refunded in US dollars to the same card. Timing depends on your bank; exchange-rate differences and bank fees are outside FNX DEV's control;
  • Brazilian methods: card refunds go to the card statement, Pix to the original account, and boleto refunds are paid after Stripe collects your bank details by email.

3.6 After the 7 days

After the guarantee period, cancellations follow the service agreement, taking into account the work already performed, without prejudice to your consumer rights.

3.7 Orders cancelled by us

If we cannot serve an order (e.g. false information or a restricted country), the full amount is refunded.
